# Content Cluster Mapping

Last updated: July 2026  
Use stage: Master Content Drafter only  
Status: Working draft for review

## Purpose

This file maps On-us content clusters based on:

- The existing LinkedIn content record / planner files
- The Content Brainstorming Quick Reference
- The previous Blog / LinkedIn / News audit
- The Master Content Generator logic

This file is not a product knowledge file. It should help the Master Content Drafter decide what type of master content to produce before repurposing into Blog, LinkedIn, News, EDM, Newsletter, or Webflow.

The key idea is:

```text
Content request
-> content type / cluster
-> strategic pillar
-> target audience / vertical
-> enterprise objective
-> relevant product / solution files
-> proof / case / claim boundary
-> master content draft
```

## Recommended Final Content Clusters

### 1. Thought Leadership

Purpose:

Shape how the market thinks and establish On-us' point of view.

Use when the content is about:

- Incentive Intelligence
- The future of loyalty
- AI in customer engagement
- Why incentives are becoming data infrastructure
- Why On-us does not fit only one category
- Market direction, category creation, or leadership perspective

Primary pillars:

- Incentive Intelligence
- Omni-Tech
- Loyalty Economy Ahead
- Last-Mile Behavioral Data

Typical target audience:

- C-level executives
- Heads of marketing / loyalty
- Investors
- Industry peers
- Senior BFSI, insurance, property, retail, or travel stakeholders

Master content must include:

- Industry context / why now
- Core market tension
- On-us point of view
- Business implication for the target audience
- Relevant product or platform logic
- Proof point or case direction, if available
- Clear claim boundary, especially for AI / roadmap claims

Avoid:

- Writing as a feature list
- Using empty AI buzzwords without explaining the mechanism
- Overstating future-facing AI capability as fully live

### 2. Educational / Explainer

Purpose:

Teach the reader how a product, mechanism, or campaign model works.

Use when the content is about:

- What Smart E-Voucher is
- How card-linked rewards work
- What VOP means
- How last-mile behavioral signals are captured
- How On-us Form, Voucher Pack, or Lucky Draw works
- How a campaign journey runs from delivery to redemption

Primary pillars:

- Card-Linked Infrastructure
- Last-Mile Behavioral Data
- Vertical Wins

Typical target audience:

- Campaign managers
- Product managers
- Loyalty teams
- Marketing operations teams
- Bank / insurer campaign teams

Master content must include:

- Plain definition
- What problem it solves
- Who uses it
- Workflow / mechanism
- Business value
- What it is not
- Relevant product KB file
- Usage limitations and claim boundaries

Avoid:

- Turning explanation into sales copy too early
- Assuming every campaign has every advanced feature
- Explaining a product without checking the product KB file

### 3. Product / Feature

Purpose:

Explain a specific On-us product capability and connect it to a business use case.

Use when the content is about:

- Smart E-Voucher
- On-us Intelligence
- VOP
- VAS
- On-us Express
- Green & ESG Solution
- Voucher Pack / Lucky Draw / On-us Form

Primary pillars:

- Card-Linked Infrastructure
- Vertical Wins
- Last-Mile Behavioral Data
- ESG / Purpose-Led Incentives

Typical target audience:

- Product managers
- Campaign leads
- Enterprise buyers evaluating tools
- Marketing and loyalty operations teams

Master content must include:

- Product definition
- Product boundary
- Target use case
- Workflow
- Feature-to-business-value mapping
- Proof point, if relevant
- Open questions if product status is unclear

Avoid:

- Product claims not supported by Tier 2 files
- Positioning VAS as the core platform
- Describing On-us Express as an enterprise custom solution

### 4. Proof / Case Study / Vertical Win

Purpose:

Show relevance through a real or anonymized example.

Use when the content is about:

- Client success
- Vertical proof
- Campaign mechanism proof
- Award / recognition as supporting proof
- Case-backed sales content

Primary pillars:

- Vertical Wins
- Card-Linked Infrastructure
- ESG / Purpose-Led Incentives

Typical target audience:

- Prospects in the same vertical
- Buyers seeking validation
- Sales teams preparing vertical-specific content
- Internal stakeholders checking proof availability

Master content must include:

- Vertical
- Region
- Scenario
- Client objective
- Challenge
- Execution process
- Redemption / fulfillment mechanism
- Impact
- Approval status
- Privacy usage note
- Safe sentence and avoid sentence

Avoid:

- Pairing sensitive client names with metrics unless approved
- Reusing old or backup examples as flagship proof
- Treating proposal / POC context as completed case study

### 5. Data / Insight

Purpose:

Lead with what On-us can learn from reward and redemption behavior.

Use when the content is about:

- 20+ behavioral signals
- Redemption behavior
- Merchant selection
- View-time / click / open behavior
- Retargeting
- Customer segmentation
- On-us Intelligence
- Data flywheel / last-mile data

Primary pillars:

- Last-Mile Behavioral Data
- Incentive Intelligence
- Loyalty Economy Ahead

Typical target audience:

- Data-minded marketers
- CMOs
- Loyalty teams
- Investors
- Marketing analytics teams

Master content must include:

- Data source / where the signal comes from
- Business question the signal answers
- What the marketer can do with the insight
- Claim scope hierarchy
- Privacy-safe wording
- On-us Intelligence status boundary

Avoid:

- Framing On-us as selling raw consumer data
- Surveillance-like language
- Using company-level scale as campaign-level performance proof

### 6. Market / Trend

Purpose:

React to external market changes through the On-us lens.

Use when the content is about:

- APAC loyalty trends
- Cross-border travel / spending
- FinTech, PayTech, MarTech, or AI market shifts
- Stablecoins / payments / digital wallets
- Industry reports
- Seasonal market timing

Primary pillars:

- Loyalty Economy Ahead
- Omni-Tech
- Card-Linked Infrastructure
- Incentive Intelligence

Typical target audience:

- Investors
- Industry watchers
- Senior marketers
- BFSI / travel / retail decision makers

Master content must include:

- External trend or trigger
- Why the trend matters now
- On-us interpretation
- Relevant vertical impact
- Product / solution connection
- Proof or market reference
- Claim boundary

Avoid:

- Writing generic market commentary without tying back to On-us capability
- Using external trend claims without source or context
- Overclaiming On-us ownership of a market trend

### 7. Brand / Values / ESG

Purpose:

Build trust around who On-us is and what kind of ecosystem it wants to create.

Use when the content is about:

- ESG and paperless incentives
- Green / ESG / Wellness Voucher

- Sustainability
- Inclusive employment
- Community impact

Primary pillars:

- ESG / Purpose-Led Incentives
- Omni-Tech
- Vertical Wins

Typical target audience:

- Broad brand audience
- Property and corporate buyers
- ESG-conscious clients
- Insurance wellness teams
- HR / employee engagement teams

Master content must include:

- Value / purpose angle
- Business relevance
- Product or campaign mechanism
- Impact proof, if approved
- ESG claim boundary
- Safe wording for carbon or sustainability claims

Avoid:

- Treating every ESG-related claim as carbon offset
- Mixing donation, tree planting, VCU retirement, and digital voucher CO2 saving into one generic claim
- Making sustainability leadership claims from recognition alone

### 8. Conversion / CTA / Sales-Generating Content

## Cluster Selection Rules

### Start from the content objective

Use this decision path:

```text
If the goal is to shape market thinking -> Thought Leadership
If the goal is to teach how something works -> Educational / Explainer
If the goal is to explain a specific capability -> Product / Feature
If the goal is to prove relevance with evidence -> Proof / Case Study / Vertical Win
If the goal is to lead with data or behavioral insight -> Data / Insight
If the goal is to react to a market shift -> Market / Trend
If the goal is to show values / ESG / trust -> Brand / Values / ESG
If the goal is to drive action -> Conversion / CTA
If the goal is to announce or recap -> Event / Award / Milestone
If the goal is employer brand or community -> Talent / Culture / Community
```

### Then select the strategic pillar

The same cluster can use different pillars.

Example:

```text
Cluster: Thought Leadership
Pillar option A: Incentive Intelligence
Pillar option B: Loyalty Economy Ahead
Pillar option C: Last-Mile Behavioral Data
```

### Then select the vertical / TA

Use:

```text
enterprise_objectives_by_vertical.md
```

The vertical controls:

- Buyer pain point
- Enterprise objective
- Commercial application
- Relevant proof / case direction
- Product file routing

### Then select product and proof files

Use the KB routing logic:

```text
Tier 1 foundation files
-> Relevant Tier 2 product files
-> Case study / proof point / partner governance files when needed
-> Master Content Drafter context
```

- Did the Drafter choose the right cluster?

- Did the Drafter avoid treating event / award content as product performance proof?
- Did the Drafter avoid treating culture content as direct sales content?
