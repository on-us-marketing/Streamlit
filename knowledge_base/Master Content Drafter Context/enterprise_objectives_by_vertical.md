# Enterprise Objectives by Vertical

Last updated: July 2026  
Use stage: Master Content Drafter only  
Status: Working draft for review

## Purpose

This file maps the marketer verticals On-us serves to:

- Key enterprise objectives
- Commercial applications On-us can support
- AI decision-intelligence opportunities unlocked by On-us Intelligence
- Suitable writing angles for master content drafting
- Relevant On-us product / solution context

This file should help the Master Content Drafter write from the buyer's business objective, not only from product features.

## Loading Rule

Load this file when the task involves:

- Master content drafting
- TA / vertical selection
- Writing angle planning
- Enterprise objective mapping
- Sales-generating content
- Thought leadership by buyer segment
- Use case or scenario planning

Do not load this file for simple product definition questions such as "What is Smart E-Voucher?" unless the answer needs to be tailored to a specific vertical.

## How To Use This File

The Master Content Drafter should follow this order:

```text
1. Identify target audience / vertical
2. Identify enterprise objective
3. Select commercial application / use case
4. Select relevant On-us product or solution context
5. Select supporting proof point or case study if appropriate
6. Draft in business outcome language
7. Send to Content Reviewer / Claim Checker
```

Do not write content only around product features. Frame the product as a way to help the target vertical achieve its business objective.

Example:

```text
Feature-led:
On-us provides Smart E-Vouchers with redemption tracking.

Business-outcome-led:
For mall operators, Smart E-Vouchers can help convert pass-by traffic into measurable tenant spending and campaign-level shopper insights.
```

- Digital banking engagement and adoption

Commercial applications:

- Loyalty and rewards program management
- Wealth management customer engagement

- Cross-border customer engagement solutions

AI intelligence opportunities:

- Customer intelligence and behavioral segmentation
- Customer engagement propensity modeling
- Next-best-action and personalized offer orchestration
- Customer lifetime value prediction and optimization
- Cross-sell and relationship expansion intelligence
- AI-driven marketing decision intelligence
- Agentic AI engagement optimization, if framed as future-facing or approved capability

Relevant On-us context:

- Smart E-Voucher
- On-us Intelligence
- VAS
- Case studies for banking / financial services

Possible writing angles:

- From reward distribution to customer intelligence
- Using incentives to deepen financial relationships
- Improving digital banking engagement with measurable rewards

Notes:

- Keep this broader than credit card campaigns.
- If the content is specifically about card-linked offers, cardholders, issuers, VOP, or non-cashback benefits, use the Card Schemes & Card Issuers vertical instead.
