# On-us Express

Last updated: July 2026
Status: Draft for review
Use stage: Master Content Brief

## Product Definition

On-us Express is a Hong Kong-focused self-serve e-voucher platform designed for faster and lighter campaign deployment.

It is best understood as a lighter deployment route for simple Hong Kong campaigns, while enterprise Smart E-Voucher projects may involve more customization, integration, merchant setup, and campaign design support.

English interpretation:

```text
A one-stop self-serve voucher platform designed for SMEs.
```

## Market Scope

On-us Express is currently treated as a Hong Kong-only product in the knowledge base.

Do not mention On-us Express in:

- Taiwan content
- Thailand content
- Global EN content
- Non-HK market materials

unless explicitly approved.

## Suitable Use Cases

On-us Express may be suitable for:

- SMEs
- Quick reward campaigns
- One-off marketing campaigns
- Simple digital voucher issuance
- Local Hong Kong promotions
- Lightweight customer appreciation campaigns
- Simple seasonal offers or festive voucher distribution
- SME campaigns that do not require dedicated enterprise support

## Typical Workflow

1. A local campaign owner selects the reward setup.
2. The campaign is configured with lighter operational requirements.
3. Smart E-Vouchers are issued for a simple promotion or customer engagement use case.
4. The marketer can use the campaign result for basic performance review and future planning.

## Included Service Elements

Based on the comparison page, On-us Express service elements include:

- Personalized greeting card and message
- Multi-merchant voucher selection
- Mobile device support
- One-stop lightweight voucher issuance
- Real-time voucher issuance status visibility

## Enterprise Meaning in This File

In this file, **On-us Enterprise** refers to On-us' customized enterprise voucher solution for larger B2B clients and campaign teams.

Enterprise clients may include banks, insurers, card schemes, large corporates, property developers, HR teams, event organizers, and other marketers that need more customized campaign design, merchant selection, API integration, data visibility, and dedicated client support.

Enterprise does not refer to end consumers. Consumers are the people who receive, open, select, and redeem vouchers.

Use **On-us Enterprise** when the campaign needs higher customization, integration, cross-channel setup, customer behavior insight, or dedicated support.

Use **On-us Express** when the campaign is a lighter Hong Kong-focused self-serve voucher campaign, especially for SMEs or simple local promotions.

## Difference From On-us Enterprise

On-us Express and On-us Enterprise should not be described as the same level of service.

| Area | On-us Express | On-us Enterprise |
|---|---|---|
| Target user | SMEs and lighter campaign owners | Enterprise clients and larger campaign teams |
| Service model | Self-serve / one-stop platform | Customized enterprise voucher solution |
| Campaign setup | Lightweight voucher issuance | End-to-end custom campaign setup from voucher design to merchant selection |
| Integration | Suitable for simpler setup | Can support API integration |
| Support | Lower-touch self-serve model | Dedicated client support |
| Data visibility | Voucher issuance status visibility | Real-time campaign status and customer behavior insight for campaign optimization |
