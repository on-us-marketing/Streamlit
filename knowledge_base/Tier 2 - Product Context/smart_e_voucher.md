## Product Definition

Smart E-Voucher is On-us' core branded product. It is a configurable digital reward mechanism that supports branded issuance, multi-merchant choice, redemption tracking, and behavioral signal capture across the customer engagement journey.

Use "Smart E-Voucher" when referring to the On-us product. Use lowercase "e-voucher" only for category or SEO keyword references.


## Product Boundary Rule

Smart E-Voucher supports reward issuance, redemption, merchant choice, voucher status tracking, and campaign interaction signals where configured.

Do not imply every Smart E-Voucher campaign includes every advanced signal, VCN settlement flow, card-linked mechanic, VOP integration, On-us Intelligence feature, or Green Voucher impact reporting by default.

If the content involves card-linked offers, VOP, issuer campaigns, or Visa, also read `vop.md`, `visa_governance.md`, and `second_party_approval_rules.md`.

If the content involves carbon offset, VCU retirement, tree planting, donation, or sustainability impact certification, also read `green_esg_solution.md`.

## What It Solves

Traditional voucher campaigns often stop at distribution. Marketers may know that a reward was sent, but have limited visibility into whether the consumer opened it, which reward they preferred, where redemption happened, and what follow-up action should happen next.

Smart E-Voucher is designed to make the reward journey more trackable and actionable. It connects reward issuance, consumer choice, merchant redemption, settlement support, and campaign performance visibility into one digital workflow.

## End-to-End Workflow

Typical Smart E-Voucher flow:

1. Marketer defines campaign objective, reward value, eligible audience, merchant options, and delivery channel.
2. On-us configures the branded Smart E-Voucher experience.
3. Consumers receive the voucher through an omni-channel delivery flow.
4. Consumers open the voucher and choose a preferred merchant or reward option.
5. Consumers redeem the voucher online, in-store, or through the configured redemption journey.
6. On-us captures campaign-level engagement and redemption signals.
7. Marketer uses the results to understand performance and plan retargeting or future campaigns.

## Where It Can Be Redeemed

Smart E-Vouchers can be redeemed through configured online, in-store, or hybrid redemption journeys, depending on campaign setup, market availability, merchant configuration, and product design.

Use this approved platform-level footprint when the content asks where Smart E-Vouchers can be redeemed:

```text
On-us supports a merchant network of 3,000+ merchants and 400,000+ physical store touchpoints.
```

How to frame it:

- Use 3,000+ merchants as platform / ecosystem coverage.
- Use 400,000+ physical store touchpoints as offline redemption footprint.
- Do not imply every merchant or every store touchpoint is available in every campaign, market, or reward setup.
- For a specific campaign, merchant availability must be confirmed based on the campaign's merchant list and configuration.

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Marketer Branding

Smart E-Vouchers can carry marketer branding, such as logos, banners, campaign visuals, color treatments, and brand-specific messaging.

Business value:

- Keeps the marketer visible throughout the reward journey
- Makes the voucher feel like part of the brand campaign
- Supports stronger brand recall

### 2. Omni-Channel Distribution

Smart E-Vouchers can be distributed through channels such as:

- Email
- SMS
- WhatsApp
- LINE
- In-app environments
- API and non-API integrations

Business value:

- Helps marketers reach consumers across different touchpoints
- Supports delivery tracking and campaign visibility
- Helps identify drop-off points in the reward journey

### 3. Multi-Merchant Choice

Marketers can include multiple merchant options in one Smart E-Voucher experience. Consumers can select the merchant they prefer at redemption.

Business value:

- Increases perceived reward value
- Improves redemption intent
- Reduces the risk of offering rewards consumers do not want

### 4. Settlement and Redemption Infrastructure

Smart E-Vouchers support settlement and redemption flows through On-us infrastructure. Some implementations may involve Virtual Card Number settlement or card-linked payment mechanics depending on campaign setup and product configuration.

Use technical details only when relevant to the audience.

### 5. Behavioral Signal Capture

Smart E-Voucher interactions can generate structured campaign signals, such as:

- Delivery status
- Open and click behavior
- Device and browser data
- Merchant selection
- Redemption timestamp
- Store or channel of redemption
- Residual payment behavior, where applicable

Business value:

- Helps marketers understand campaign engagement
- Supports future segmentation and retargeting
- Gives visibility beyond simple voucher issuance

### 6. Web-Based Redemption Experience

Smart E-Vouchers are web-based. Consumers can open and redeem them through a voucher link without downloading a separate app.

Typical online redemption:

1. Consumer opens the Smart E-Voucher link.
2. Consumer selects a merchant or reward option.
3. Consumer clicks redeem.
4. Consumer receives the relevant voucher code or redemption instruction.
5. Voucher status updates after redemption.

Typical in-store redemption:

1. Consumer opens the Smart E-Voucher link.
2. Consumer selects the preferred merchant or reward option.
3. Consumer presents the unique QR code or redemption interface at checkout.
4. Merchant completes the redemption according to the configured flow.
5. Voucher status updates after redemption.

Business value:

- Removes app download friction
- Makes redemption easier across online and offline journeys
- Supports clearer status tracking for consumers and marketers

### 7. Flexible Reward Design

Smart E-Vouchers can support different reward configurations depending on campaign setup.

Examples include:

- Different voucher values
- Customizable denominations
- Multi-currency campaign design, where configured
- Different validity periods
- Online or in-store redemption routes
- Product-based rewards, cash-value rewards, or mixed reward options, where supported

Business value:

- Allows marketers to match reward mechanics with campaign goals
- Supports both local and cross-border reward use cases
- Gives consumers more relevant reward choices

## Comparison With Cashback

Smart E-Voucher should not be positioned as simply another discount or cashback mechanic.

Compared with a cashback-only reward, Smart E-Voucher can support:

- Better brand visibility inside the reward journey
- Merchant choice and reward personalization
- Faster digital fulfillment
- Campaign-level behavioral signal capture
- Retargeting and follow-up planning after redemption

Do not claim that Smart E-Voucher always outperforms cashback. Use enabling language such as "can support", "helps marketers", or "is designed to".
