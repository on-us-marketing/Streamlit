# Green & ESG Solution

Last updated: July 2026
Use stage: Master Content Brief

## Purpose

Green & ESG Solution is the umbrella solution file for sustainability-linked Smart E-Voucher campaigns.

It contains three closely related sales and marketing angles:

1. Green Voucher
2. ESG Voucher
3. Wellness Voucher

They share the same underlying Smart E-Voucher mechanism, but each angle is used for a different campaign story, buyer need, and customer motivation.

## Core Definition

On-us Green & ESG Solution connects Smart E-Voucher rewards with ESG values, including sustainable merchants, social enterprise rewards, wellness benefits, and carbon-offsetting or green contribution options.

The core idea is to help marketers turn a reward campaign into a visible ESG engagement journey. Instead of only giving a cash reward or generic voucher, the end user can select a sustainability-related reward option, while the marketer connects the campaign to brand purpose, ESG positioning, and measurable engagement.

## Shared Product Foundation

All three angles are built on Smart E-Voucher and can share the following foundation:

- Digital reward delivery
- Marketer-branded voucher page
- Multi-option reward selection
- Sustainable merchant, ESG merchant, wellness, or green project options
- Redemption tracking
- Campaign-level engagement data
- Optional contribution or impact reporting, depending on campaign setup

## Smart E-Voucher Carbon-Saving Baseline

Each Smart E-Voucher can use the following approved sustainability proof point when the content is about digital voucher format, paperless reward delivery, ESG messaging, or carbon-saving comparison:

```text
ISO 14067:2018 Certified
Savings: 12.2g CO2 per Smart E-Voucher
```

This should be framed as the carbon saving of the Smart E-Voucher digital format compared with a paper voucher baseline.

Do not confuse this proof point with:

- Carbon offset
- VCU retirement
- Tree planting
- Donation amount
- Green project contribution
- Campaign-specific impact certification

For Green Voucher or ESG Voucher content, this 12.2g CO2 proof point can support the paperless digital voucher angle, but project-level impact claims still depend on the actual campaign setup and approved reporting method.


Strong boundary:

- 12.2g CO2 savings belongs to each Smart E-Voucher as a digital-format carbon-saving comparison.
- It is not the same as Green Voucher carbon offset, ESG Voucher donation, VCU retirement, tree planting, environmental project contribution, or campaign-specific impact certification.
- If both the 12.2g proof point and Green / ESG / Wellness angles appear in the same brief, separate them clearly under different claim scopes.


## Angle Separation Rule

The AI should pick one primary angle before drafting:

- Wellness Voucher: wellbeing, healthy lifestyle, fitness, wellness products, insurance wellness engagement, employee wellbeing.
- ESG Voucher: social impact, social enterprise, community, inclusion, donation, responsible merchant choices.
- Green Voucher: carbon, verified environmental projects, VCU retirement, tree planting, green finance, Verra, Gold Standard.

Do not reposition an insurance case as a Green / ESG / Wellness case unless the case itself is approved for that exact angle.

BOC Life can be used as an approved insurance loyalty and wellness reward fulfillment case. Do not use it as proof of Green Voucher impact, ESG Voucher impact, lead conversion, policy activation, renewal improvement, or LTV uplift unless separately approved.

CONFIRM-only Green / ESG / Wellness cases should stay in Open Questions or internal notes unless re-approved.

## Three Sales Angles

| Angle | Best used when the campaign is about | Main customer motivation | Typical buyer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Green Voucher | Green finance, environmental contribution, eco-friendly project support, carbon credit retirement | "My reward contributes to the environment" | Banks, insurers, green finance teams, ESG product teams |

| Wellness Voucher | Healthy living, fitness, lifestyle, wellness products, employee wellbeing | "My reward supports personal wellbeing" | HR teams, insurers, wellness marketers, corporate engagement teams |

## How AI Should Choose the Angle

Use **Green Voucher** when the content focuses on:

- Green mortgages
- Green loans
- EV-related finance
- Eco-friendly projects
- Verra or Gold Standard certification
- Environmental contribution or post-campaign impact reporting

Use **ESG Voucher** when the content focuses on:

- Social enterprise support
- Community growth
- Inclusion
- Donation
- Responsible merchant partnerships
- ESG brand commitments
- Corporate social responsibility campaigns

Use **Wellness Voucher** when the content focuses on:

- Health and wellness
- Fitness or yoga experience
- Natural health products
- Employee wellbeing
- Insurance wellness engagement
- Lifestyle-related benefits

If the topic is broad or strategic, use **Green & ESG Solution** as the umbrella term.

## Shared User Journey

Typical Green & ESG Solution journey:

1. The marketer defines the campaign objective, such as green finance activation, ESG engagement, employee wellbeing, or customer loyalty.
2. The consumer receives a Smart E-Voucher through an omni-channel delivery flow, such as SMS, email, chatbot, or in-app channel.
3. The consumer opens the voucher without needing to download a separate app.
4. The voucher page presents green, ESG, wellness, or sustainable merchant options.
5. The consumer selects and redeems the preferred reward.
6. On-us supports the redemption and settlement workflow.
7. Campaign-level engagement and redemption data can support performance review and future campaign planning.
8. Post-campaign reporting may include contribution or certification details when relevant.

## Green Voucher

### Positioning

Green Voucher helps marketers connect incentives with environmental contribution and green finance storytelling.

Use this angle when the campaign needs to support green finance, eco-friendly projects, environmental contribution, or environmental protection.

### Common Campaign Use Cases

- Green mortgage campaigns
- Green personal loan campaigns
- Electric vehicle loan promotion
- Green residential project refinancing
- Sustainability-linked loan campaigns
- ESG investment or low-carbon fund engagement campaigns
- Environmental contribution reward campaigns

### Common Reward / Project Options

- Carbon credit retirement
- Tree planting contribution
- Environmental donation
- EV charging credit
- Eco-friendly project contribution
- Verra or Gold Standard certified project options

### Proof and Reporting Angle

Post-campaign certification can be included as an optional function. It may show contribution information such as:

- Number of VCUs retired
- Number of trees planted
- Donation amount
- Campaign-specific environmental contribution, when the campaign has an approved calculation or reporting setup

This does not need to be mentioned in every content copy. Use it when the content angle is about ESG contribution, reporting, proof of impact, or post-campaign accountability.

### Green Impact Reporting Boundary

Green Voucher impact reporting should focus on approved project contribution, redemption setting, contribution method, or campaign-specific reporting setup.

The 12.2g CO2 per Smart E-Voucher proof point can be used when the content is about paperless digital voucher delivery. However, the AI should not turn this into a Green Voucher carbon offset, VCU retirement, tree planting, or donation claim.

Use this field in the master brief:

```text
Green impact or contribution:
Certification:
Project or contribution source:
Can be used externally: Yes / No / Approval required
```

Safe wording:

```text
Each campaign can define an environmental contribution or project impact when there is an approved reporting setup.
```

Avoid using the 12.2g CO2 per Smart E-Voucher proof point as the basis for a campaign-specific Green Voucher impact claim unless the campaign has an approved calculation or reporting setup.

## ESG Voucher

### Positioning

ESG Voucher helps marketers connect rewards with social impact and responsible partnerships.

Use this angle when the campaign is more about community, inclusion, social enterprises, responsible merchant partnerships, or ESG brand commitment rather than direct carbon offset.

### Common Campaign Use Cases

- Corporate CSR campaigns
- Social enterprise support campaigns
- Customer loyalty campaigns with ESG positioning
- Employee engagement campaigns with social impact angle
- Insurance or financial services campaigns linked to responsible lifestyle choices

### Common Reward / Contribution Options

- Social enterprise merchant rewards
- Donation-linked reward options
- Community support incentives
- Inclusion-related campaign rewards
- Responsible merchant choices

### Carbon-Saving Proof

Smart E-Voucher carbon-saving proof point:

```text
ISO 14067:2018 Certified
Savings: 12.2g CO2 per Smart E-Voucher
```

Use this proof point when the content is about ESG Voucher, carbon saving, paperless reward delivery, sustainability reporting, or ESG contribution.

This proof point belongs to the Smart E-Voucher digital format. It can support ESG Voucher content, but it should not be treated as a donation, carbon offset, or campaign-specific ESG impact result.

Safe wording:

```text
Each Smart E-Voucher is ISO 14067:2018 Certified and is associated with 12.2g CO2 savings per voucher compared with a paper voucher baseline.
```

Avoid:

```text
Each ESG Voucher automatically offsets 12.2g CO2.
```

Reason: the 12.2g proof point is a digital-format carbon-saving comparison, not an offset claim.

## Impact Verification

For Smart E-Voucher digital delivery, the approved carbon-saving proof point is ISO 14067:2018 Certified with 12.2g CO2 savings per Smart E-Voucher.

Impact verification can be used to explain how Green / ESG-related voucher redemption connects rewards with carbon savings, contribution recognition, and measurable sustainability outcomes.

Use impact verification carefully. It should support the campaign story, not become a broad claim that every Green / ESG / Wellness campaign has the same reporting structure.

## Relationship With Smart E-Voucher

Green & ESG Solution is a solution layer built on top of Smart E-Voucher, not a replacement for Smart E-Voucher.

Smart E-Voucher provides:

- Digital reward delivery
- Voucher branding
- Multi-option selection
- Redemption flow
- Settlement support
- Behavioral signal capture

Green & ESG Solution adds:

- Sustainability-related sales angle
- Green, ESG, or wellness reward options
- ESG merchant or carbon project choices
- Optional certification or contribution reporting
- Brand purpose and sustainability messaging
