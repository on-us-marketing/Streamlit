# Visa Offer Platform (VOP)

Last updated: July 2026
Status: Draft for review
Use stage: Master Content Brief

## Product Definition

VOP refers to Visa Offer Platform. Smart E-Voucher with VOP is a card-linked, spending-based incentive capability co-created with Visa.

Use this context only when the content is about card-linked offers, cardholder engagement, bank campaigns, payment-linked rewards, or Visa-related incentive scenarios.

Approved core wording:

```text
On-us powers the Visa Offer Platform to deliver card-linked non-cashback benefits.
```

## What "Non-Cashback Benefits" Means

"Non-cashback benefits" is the term used to describe credit card rewards that are not direct cash rebate. These benefits can include merchant rewards, travel benefits, Smart E-Vouchers, lifestyle perks, and other value-added cardholder incentives.

Use this term when explaining how VOP helps issuers go beyond simple cashback offers and provide more engaging card-linked benefits.

## How It Works

At a high level:

1. A bank, issuer, or marketer configures campaign conditions.
2. Conditions may include merchant category, minimum spend, location, currency, or eligible card criteria.
3. Consumers participate through a card-linked reward journey.
4. Smart E-Vouchers can be issued after qualifying behavior or spending conditions are met.
5. The marketer receives engagement and campaign performance visibility, depending on approved data scope.


## VOP and Smart E-Voucher Boundary

VOP is the card-linked and transaction-qualification context.

Smart E-Voucher can be configured as the non-cashback reward fulfillment layer after qualification.

Do not describe Smart E-Voucher fulfillment, merchant choice, QR redemption, VCN settlement, or redemption tracking as automatic VOP functionality unless the source confirms that exact campaign setup.

Use enabling language:

```text
Smart E-Voucher can be configured as the non-cashback benefit issued after a transaction qualifies through VOP.
```

Avoid:

```text
VOP automatically gives cardholders multi-merchant Smart E-Voucher choice.
```


## Card Issuer / VOP Generation Checklist

For card issuer, card scheme, overseas spending, or non-cashback benefit content:

- Use `On-us powers the Visa Offer Platform to deliver card-linked non-cashback benefits` only in VOP / card-linked context.
- Describe Smart E-Voucher as a configurable fulfillment layer, not as an automatic VOP function.
- If mentioning merchant choice, QR redemption, VCN settlement, or redemption tracking, clearly attribute those to Smart E-Voucher and say `where configured`.
- If using DBS, enrollment, overseas dining spend, or Visa x client examples, check `case_studies.md`, `claim_scope_hierarchy.md`, `visa_governance.md`, and `second_party_approval_rules.md`.
- Do not pair DBS with CONFIRM enrollment or spending metrics in external copy unless re-approved.

## Business Value

VOP-related Smart E-Voucher campaigns can help banks and card schemes:

- Encourage card usage
- Influence cardholder payment choice
- Drive cross-border spending engagement
- Support travel and merchant reward campaigns
- Create non-cashback cardholder benefits
- Build richer reward experiences around merchant categories, travel behavior, and cardholder lifecycle moments
