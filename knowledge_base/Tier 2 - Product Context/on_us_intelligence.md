# On-us Intelligence

Last updated: July 2026
Status: Draft for review
Use stage: Master Content Brief

## Product Definition

On-us Intelligence is the data and intelligence layer that helps marketers understand reward campaign performance, consumer engagement behavior, redemption patterns, and future optimization opportunities.

## Enterprise Meaning in This File

In this file, **enterprise** means On-us' B2B clients or campaign owners, such as banks, insurers, card schemes, corporates, property developers, HR teams, event organizers, and large-scale marketers.

Enterprise does not mean the end consumer receiving the voucher. It also does not mean "enterprise software" in a generic SaaS sense. In On-us content, enterprise refers to the buyer or campaign owner that uses On-us to design, distribute, redeem, and analyze incentive campaigns.

When writing content, use:

- enterprise clients
- enterprise marketers
- campaign owners
- B2B marketers
- enterprise campaign owners

Do not use enterprise as a vague buzzword. Tie it to actual campaign owners who need customer engagement, reward distribution, analytics, and optimization support.

Use **enterprise marketers** when the sentence is about marketing teams, campaign teams, banks, insurers, or corporates using On-us.

Use **consumers** when the sentence is about people receiving, opening, selecting, or redeeming vouchers.

Use **merchants** when the sentence is about redemption partners.

## What It Solves

Many reward campaigns can report basic delivery or redemption numbers, but marketers often need a clearer view of the full journey: who opened the reward, which reward option they selected, when they redeemed, which merchant category performed better, and how the campaign should be improved next time.

On-us Intelligence is designed to turn Smart E-Voucher interactions into campaign-level behavioral signals that can support analysis, segmentation, retargeting, and future campaign planning.

## Important Status Rule

On-us Intelligence should be treated as **partially live**.

### Currently Live / Available

The current offering includes:

- Campaign analytics
- 20+ behavioral signals
- Campaign performance visibility
- Engagement and redemption-related metrics
- Data foundation for better campaign decisions

### Future-Facing / Not Automatically Live

The following should be treated as future-facing or next evolution unless confirmed:

- Full agentic AI layer
- 5-layer AI architecture as a fully deployed product
- Conversational Loyalty Copilot
- Autonomous loyalty operations
- AI agents that independently execute campaign optimization
- Natural language campaign management
- Pre-launch simulation as a live commercial feature

## Suggested Safe Wording

Use:

```text
On-us Intelligence currently helps enterprise marketers analyze reward campaign performance through 20+ behavioral signals. Broader agentic AI capabilities should be described as future-facing unless their live status is confirmed.
```

Avoid:

```text
On-us Intelligence fully automates loyalty decision-making through autonomous AI agents.
```


## Generation Guardrails From Testing

Current capability should be framed as:

```text
campaign analytics, behavioral signals, segmentation support, retargeting planning, and campaign performance visibility
```

Future-facing capability should be framed as:

```text
agentic AI, Conversational Loyalty Copilot, autonomous optimization, or natural-language campaign management, only when clearly marked as future-facing or pending confirmation
```

Do not write:

```text
fully autonomous loyalty operations are live
On-us Intelligence performs respondent scoring
On-us Intelligence performs fraud scoring
On-us Intelligence guarantees LTV uplift
On-us Intelligence uses surveillance tracking
```

Do not turn defensive privacy language into a marketing claim. Instead of saying `not surveillance`, say `campaign-level behavioral signals within approved data scope`.

When using `20+ behavioral signals`, include a reference trace to `proof_points.md`, `claim_scope_hierarchy.md`, and this file. Do not claim a specific campaign used all 20+ signals unless the case source confirms it.
