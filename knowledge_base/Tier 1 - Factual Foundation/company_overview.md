## Company Background and Market Coverage

On-us was founded in Hong Kong in 2018 by Dennis Shi, Patrick Lee, and Honnus Cheung.

The company operates as a Hong Kong-founded global Incentive Intelligence platform with market presence and campaign coverage across:

- Hong Kong
- Taiwan
- Singapore
- Malaysia
- Thailand

On-us also supports cross-border and regional reward campaigns through its merchant and redemption ecosystem. For broad company-scale content, use approved proof points such as 50M+ consumer reach, 3,000+ merchants, and 400,000+ physical store touchpoints only when the content needs scale credibility.

Use this section for factual company background. Do not overstate that every product, merchant, or campaign setup is available equally in every market.

## Vision and Mission

On-us was founded to reignite customer engagement and help businesses grow through smarter incentive experiences.

The company believes incentives are more than transactions. They can connect marketers, merchants, and consumers through moments of reward, redemption, and measurable engagement.

The brand idea "For you, On-us" reflects the company's commitment to creating value for enterprise clients and their end consumers.

## Who On-us Serves

On-us serves enterprise clients, also referred to as marketers, across sectors including:

- Banking and financial services
- Insurance
- Card schemes
- Corporate HR and employee engagement
- Property developers
- FMCG and large brands
- Events, MICE, and community campaigns
- SMEs through selected offerings such as On-us Express

## What On-us Enables

On-us helps enterprise marketers:

- Issue Smart E-Vouchers across multiple channels
- Offer consumers a choice of merchants
- Track voucher delivery, opening, selection, redemption, and post-campaign behavior
- Support card-linked or spending-based incentives through VOP
- Run employee, event, loyalty, acquisition, and retention campaigns
- Turn reward interactions into usable campaign intelligence
