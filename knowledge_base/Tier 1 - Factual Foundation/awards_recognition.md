# Awards and Recognition

Last updated: July 2026
Use stage: Load only when awards or recognition proof is needed

## Purpose

This file stores On-us awards, recognitions, and accelerator records separately from the main company overview. It should not be loaded for every content request because award lists can consume unnecessary context.

Load this file only when the content needs credibility proof, investor-facing positioning, company profile content, award submission, partnership deck copy, or a milestone announcement.

## Usage Rule

Use awards and recognition as credibility support, not as the main sales message.

Do:

- Use awards to support company credibility.
- Keep the wording factual.
- Match the award to the relevant topic when possible.
- Separate awards from accelerator program participation.
- Use status carefully.

Do not:

- Turn awards into unsupported superlatives.
- Say On-us is "#1" unless that exact claim is approved.
- Use every award in one content piece.
- Mix sustainability recognition into product functionality claims.
- Present "Joined" or "Awaiting results" records as awards won.

## Records Not To Phrase As Awards

The CSV also contains entries with statuses such as "Joined" or "Awaiting results". These may be useful internally for tracking, but they should not be presented as awards or recognitions won.

Examples:

- NTT Startup Challenge 2025, Joined
- EY Tokyo Financial Award, Awaiting results
- Hysan LMTP, Awaiting results
- SusHi Tech Challenge, Joined
- DBS SME Grant 2022, Joined
- Marketing Excellence Awards 2021, Joined

## Example Safe Wording

```text
On-us has been recognized by regional FinTech, payment, retail technology, and innovation programs, supporting its positioning as a technology-led Incentive Intelligence platform.
```

```text
On-us was named the "Loyalty of The Future" category winner in the Visa Accelerator Programme APAC, reinforcing its relevance in card-linked loyalty and payment-linked incentive scenarios.
```

```text
On-us was recognized by IFTA for FinTech with ESG Initiative of the Year, which can support ESG-related positioning when discussing Green & ESG Solution.
```

## Avoid

```text
On-us is the leading Green Voucher platform because it received ESG-related recognition.
```

Reason: the recognition can support credibility, but it should not be used as proof of market leadership or product performance.

```text
On-us won every accelerator listed in the tracker.
```

Reason: some records are accelerator participation or joined status, not awards won.
