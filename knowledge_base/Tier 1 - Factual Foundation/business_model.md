## Business Model Summary

On-us operates as a B2B2C incentive ecosystem connecting:

1. **Enterprise marketers** who want to engage, acquire, retain, or reward target audiences
2. **Merchant partners** who provide redemption options and benefit from campaign-driven traffic or spending
3. **End consumers** who receive, open, select, and redeem Smart E-Vouchers

The core business logic is:

```text
Enterprise demand for engagement
-> Smart E-Voucher campaign design and distribution
-> Consumer reward selection and redemption
-> Merchant traffic or spending opportunity
-> Campaign-level data and insights for marketers
```

In On-us content, **incentive ecosystem** should refer to this connected value flow. It should not be used as a generic synonym for reward program, loyalty program, or marketing campaign.

## Stakeholder Roles

### 1. Enterprise Marketers

Enterprise marketers are On-us' B2B clients and campaign owners. They are the organizations that use On-us to design, distribute, manage, and evaluate incentive campaigns.

Typical enterprise marketers include:

- Banks and card issuers
- Insurance companies
- Card schemes
- Corporate HR and employee engagement teams
- FMCG and large consumer brands
- Property developers
- Event, MICE, and community campaign organizers
- Research or survey-led campaign owners
- SMEs in selected Hong Kong use cases through On-us Express

Enterprise marketers may use On-us for:

- Customer acquisition
- Lead generation
- Customer retention and reactivation
- Cardholder engagement
- Cross-border travel incentives
- Employee rewards and benefit programs
- Event engagement and prize fulfillment
- Survey or form-linked incentive campaigns
- Green, ESG, or wellness-related reward campaigns

What enterprise marketers need from On-us:

- A branded reward experience
- Access to merchant reward options
- Campaign setup and distribution support
- A smoother redemption journey for consumers
- Less manual reward operation work
- Campaign performance visibility
- Behavioral signals that support future segmentation, retargeting, or campaign optimization

Business value for enterprise marketers:

- Improve reward engagement
- Increase redemption intent through merchant choice
- Reduce operational burden in reward fulfillment
- Track the incentive journey more clearly
- Support measurable incentive ROI
- Turn reward interactions into campaign-level insights

Do not confuse enterprise marketers with end consumers. Enterprise marketers are the buyers or campaign owners; consumers are the people receiving and redeeming the reward.

### 2. Merchant Partners

Merchants are redemption partners in the On-us ecosystem. They provide the products, services, or locations where consumers can redeem Smart E-Vouchers.

Merchants can benefit from:

- Campaign-driven exposure
- Additional consumer traffic
- Participation in multi-merchant reward programs
- Potential residual spending beyond voucher value
- Access to new customer groups through enterprise campaigns

When writing about merchants, do not imply that every merchant is available in every market or every campaign. Merchant availability depends on campaign setup, market, merchant category, and product configuration.

### 3. End Consumers

Consumers are the end users who receive, open, select, and redeem Smart E-Vouchers.

Consumers are not On-us' direct B2B clients. Their role in the ecosystem is to engage with the reward journey and create campaign interaction signals through actions such as:

- opening the voucher
- selecting a merchant or reward option
- redeeming the reward
- completing a form, survey, event flow, or campaign action where relevant
- making residual payment or spending where applicable

Consumer engagement should be described as campaign-level interaction signals, not as raw data extraction or surveillance.

## Value Flow

```text
Enterprise marketer defines campaign objective
-> On-us supports reward design, setup, and distribution
-> Consumer receives and opens the Smart E-Voucher
-> Consumer selects a merchant or reward option
-> Consumer redeems the reward
-> Merchant receives traffic or spending opportunity
-> Marketer receives campaign performance visibility and behavioral insights
-> Marketer can plan follow-up, retargeting, or future campaign optimization
```

## How AI Should Explain the Business Model

Good explanation:

```text
On-us connects enterprise marketers, merchants, and consumers through Smart E-Voucher campaigns. Marketers can distribute branded incentives, consumers can select and redeem preferred rewards, and merchants can receive campaign-driven traffic. The reward journey also creates campaign-level engagement signals that help marketers review performance and plan future campaigns.
```

Avoid:

```text
On-us sells customer data to help brands target users.
```

Reason:

This creates raw data sale and surveillance framing. The correct framing is campaign-level insight and better incentive decision-making.

## Important Framing Rules

Do:

- Explain the three-party value flow clearly.
- Keep enterprise marketers, merchants, and consumers separate.
- Connect incentives with engagement, redemption, and campaign insight.
- Use product files when explaining detailed product mechanics.
- Use proof point and case study files before adding numbers or examples.

Do not:

- Use "incentive ecosystem" as a generic term for any reward program.
- Present consumers as On-us' direct B2B clients.
- Describe On-us as a raw data seller.
- Invent pricing, commission, revenue-share, or commercial terms not provided in the knowledge base.
- Claim every campaign includes the same merchants, data outputs, or product features.
